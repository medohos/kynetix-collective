Frequently Asked Questions

Are all products 100% authentic? ↓

Yes, absolutely. We source exclusively from certified regional and international distributors for Nike, Adidas, Puma, Under Armour, and Reebok. Every pair of shoes comes in its original retail box with intact SKU codes and authenticity tags.

How long does delivery take across Egypt? ↓

Orders are dispatched from our central Cairo hub within 24 hours:

  • Cairo & Giza: 1 to 2 business days. Priority express courier available for next-day delivery.
  • Alexandria & Delta Governorates: 2 to 3 business days.
  • Red Sea, Upper Egypt & Coastal Cities: 3 to 4 business days.

Standard shipping is completely FREE for all orders over 1000 EGP.

What is your 14-day exchange and return policy? ↓

If your shoes or sportswear do not fit perfectly, you can request a size exchange or full refund within 14 days of delivery. The item must be unworn, in its original box with tags attached. You can initiate an exchange via our Contact Form or directly via WhatsApp.

What payment methods do you accept? ↓

We accept:

  • Credit & Debit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, and Egyptian Meeza cards.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Pay cash in hand when your package arrives at your doorstep.
  • InstaPay & Mobile Wallets: Instant direct transfer via InstaPay (kynetix@instapay) or Vodafone Cash / Orange Money.
How can I track my shipment? ↓

Simply navigate to our Track Order Page and enter your order number (e.g. KYN-842910) and your mobile number. You will see real-time updates as your gear moves from Cairo fulfillment to courier dispatch.
